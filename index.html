Why is the Facilities category generating only 4% savings?
09:22 AM — Solution Architect
Facilities Management savings are set at 4% (Typical, North America) per the GCL benchmark for Cleaning & Janitorial, which reflects the high market consolidation in this category.

The addressability is 72%, giving $21.5M of the $29.8M spend in scope. The GCL factor also reflects that this is a mature category with limited re-sourcing leverage beyond the first cycle — most service providers operate on thin margins and large incumbents have significant retention advantages.

You can override this to the High benchmark (6%) if the client has not competitively sourced in the last 36 months. Navigate to Screen 4 → Strategic Sourcing and click Override on the Cleaning & Janitorial row.

What happens if I delay Wave 2 by 3 months?
09:31 AM — Solution Architect
Delaying Wave 2 from Month 8 to Month 11 would have the following impact on the model:

📉 Year 1 savings reduced by ~$1.2M — the 3 months of MRO and Logistics savings that would have started in months 8–10 would not be realized in the first year.

📊 Year 2 savings remain largely unchanged — full-year Wave 2 savings still flow through in Year 2, just starting 3 months later.

📉 Total contract savings decrease by ~$0.8M due to the shorter realization window in Year 3 as the second MRO sourcing cycle would fall partially outside the contract term.

New total (Typical scenario): ~$33.4M vs $34.2M current.

Click 'Apply Change' in Screen 5 → Wave Planning to update the model with this timing change.
